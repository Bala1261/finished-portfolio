BEXO Content & Professional Portfolio Standard

A rigorous visual and architectural standard for Content Strategists, Writers, Consultants, and Thought Leaders. Preserves the BEXO 4-route contract, runtime data injection, accessible navigation, and verified platform handoff.

Reference: Template-03 Content & Professional Four public routes Data-driven rendering Responsive + Accessible

1. Product purpose

Every portfolio must immediately help a client, collaborator, or recruiter answer four questions: who is this thinker, what proves their strategic capability, how can I read their work or contact them, and how can I retain or hire them?

System rule New templates may change the visual language, but they must preserve the public-site contract. The BEXO renderer must inject the same profile data and the shared navigation must route identically.
Do not Add a second competing primary navigation, hard-code user identity into production files, or duplicate the platform Hire Me workflow inside a template.

Portfolio

Order: Identity Block → Capabilities & Skills → Case Studies → Published Essays → Experience Timeline → Education → Certificates → Distinctions → Endorsements. Empty sections are cleanly hidden without orphan headings.

Contact

Required behavior Direct mailto email link, truthful status pill bound solely to openToHire, and form validation (name, email, message, optional phone, honeypot). Submits to /api/profile/public/{handle}/contact with accessible aria-live feedback.

Hire Me

Platform handoff Single primary action resolving to /hire-me/{handle}. 3 capability pillars (Brand Voice, Executive Ghostwriting, Editorial Systems). Direct email/phone fallback links.

6. Acceptance checklist

  • Home renders name, designation, portrait, proof metrics, and View Portfolio CTA.
  • Resume button renders only when a valid resumeUrl exists.
  • Portfolio cleanly hides empty collections without blank titles or awkward layout gaps.
  • Article reading modal opens on essay click with a reading progress bar and pull-quote styling.
  • Case study modal presents challenges, strategic approach, and measurable outcomes.
  • Contact reflects truthful openToHire: true and false states.
  • Contact form validates input, suppresses bot spam via honeypot, and delivers accessible live feedback.
  • Hire Me routes cleanly to the shared platform endpoint with fallback direct contacts.
  • Responsive layouts tested at 320px, 375px, 768px, 992px, and desktop widths without horizontal scroll.
  • Color themes (Warm Paper light mode and Editorial dark mode) toggle smoothly and persist.
  • Vite multi-page production build succeeds with zero compiler warnings or errors.
