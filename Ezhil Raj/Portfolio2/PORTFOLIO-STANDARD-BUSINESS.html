Executive Business & Management Standard

A detailed visual and technical specification for public portfolios representing BBA/MBA graduates, Management Consultants, Business Analysts, and Corporate Leaders matching the BEXO standard contract.

Theme: Business & Management Four public routes window.__BEXO_PROFILE__ Data-driven rendering Accessible & Responsive

1. Product purpose

Every executive business portfolio must answer four high-stakes questions rapidly: who is this professional, what commercial/analytical outcomes prove their mastery, how can an organization contact them, and how can they be retained or hired?

System rule The template preserves the public-site contract. The BEXO platform renderer injects window.__BEXO_PROFILE__ dynamically, and the 4 canonical routes handle navigation identically.
Do not Hard-code user identity into production files, omit required routes, or duplicate platform hiring forms.

3. Data contract

Production receives normalized profile objects through window.__BEXO_PROFILE__. Local preview is provided by js/profile-data.js, and normalized by js/profile-runtime.js.

{
  "user": {
    "name": "David Vance",
    "email": "david.vance.biz@gmail.com",
    "photoUrl": "assets/executive_portrait.jpg",
    "resumeUrl": "assets/David_Vance_Executive_Resume.pdf",
    "openToHire": true,
    "phone": "+1 (212) 555-0194",
    "location": "New York, NY • Open to Relocation"
  },
  "profile": {
    "handle": "davidvance",
    "headline": "Senior Business Analyst & Strategy Consultant",
    "careerGoal": "Bridging corporate strategy and rigorous, data-driven analytical execution.",
    "bio": "I combine business thinking, analytical skills and practical execution..."
  },
  "projectEntries": [...],
  "experienceEntries": [...],
  "educationEntries": [...],
  "certificateEntries": [...],
  "achievementEntries": [...],
  "researchEntries": [...],
  "skillEntries": [...]
}

Normalized collections

Consumes projectEntries, experienceEntries, educationEntries, certificateEntries, achievementEntries, researchEntries, and skillEntries as arrays.

Empty collection rule

Renders zero, one, or many items. If an array is empty or undefined, the section automatically hides without blank headings or gaps.

Truthful state rules user.resumeUrl controls the resume CTA button. user.openToHire === true controls the opportunity badge. Neither state is inferred from unrelated fields.

4. Page-by-page implementation rules

Home (index.html)

IdentityRequired (Name + Portrait)
Designationheadline → bio fallback
Primary ActionView Portfolio (links to pages/portfolio.html)
Resume CTAOnly visible when resumeUrl exists
Status BadgeOnly visible when openToHire === true

Portfolio (pages/portfolio.html)

Order: About / Identity → Skills → Experience → Education → Selected Work (Case Studies) → Certificates → Achievements → Research.

Interactive Case Study Lightbox

Clicking "View Case Study Breakdown" opens an accessible modal with Challenge, Research, Strategy, Solution, Quantified Commercial Outcomes, and Learnings.

Interactive Skills Tabs

Filterable by category: All, Strategy, Analytics, Leadership, Tools with proficiency levels and contextual descriptions.

Contact (pages/contact.html)

Behavioral rules Direct mailto: link, open-to-hire badge, honeypot spam protection, validation for name, email, message, and phone (when provided), submitting to /api/profile/public/{handle}/contact with aria-live feedback.

Hire Me (pages/hire-me.html)

Platform handoff Single dominant action button resolving to /hire-me/{handle} with fallback direct email and office phone actions. No redundant secondary hiring forms.

5. Visual and interaction standard

Executive Design Language

Deep corporate navy (#071426), electric cobalt (#145BFF), and light slate (#F8FAFC). Crisp typography with Plus Jakarta Sans & Inter.

Responsive Breakpoints

Desktop (1200px), Tablet (900px), Mobile (600px, 375px, 320px). Zero horizontal scroll at 320px width.

7. Accessibility and SEO

  • Single descriptive <h1> per page with tailored meta descriptions.
  • Accessible navigation with aria-current="page" and keyboard trap on mobile drawer.
  • Skip-to-content link (#main-content) at the start of every document.
  • Informative alt attributes on all portrait and dashboard media.
  • Accessible forms with explicit labels, aria-required, aria-invalid, and aria-live status.
  • Full support for prefers-reduced-motion: reduce.

8. Acceptance checklist

  • Home renders name, designation, portrait photo, and View Portfolio CTA.
  • Resume CTA button appears only with valid user.resumeUrl.
  • Status badge appears only when user.openToHire === true.
  • Portfolio renders all 8 collections cleanly, hiding any empty arrays.
  • Contact validates name, email, message, and phone; submits to /api/profile/public/{handle}/contact.
  • Contact honeypot is invisible to users and catches automated submissions.
  • Hire Me routes to /hire-me/{handle} with direct fallback channels.
  • Navigation operates seamlessly across all routes with correct active states.
  • 320px, 375px, 768px, and desktop widths have no horizontal overflow.
  • Keyboard navigation, focus rings, skip link, and reduced motion work properly.
  • Zero console runtime errors across all pages.
  • Production injection via window.__BEXO_PROFILE__ overrides local demo data.
